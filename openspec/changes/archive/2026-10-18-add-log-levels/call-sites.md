## Matched by the words, left as `info`

- `Capture retry pass finished`: Normal end of a retry pass; the word 'retry' is the topic, not a problem.
- `Auto-capture skipped`: The extractor judged the turn not worth saving. Routine.
- `Pi auto-capture skipped by extractor`: The extractor judged the turn not worth saving. Routine.
- `waiting after a failure`: Backoff wait. The failure that started it is already logged as `error`.
- `Skipping idle processing for internal capture session`: By design: stops OMMS's own sessions from re-triggering capture.
- `profile description evolution skipped: no change`: Nothing to update. Routine.
- `profile description evolution skipped: insufficient evidence`: Not enough evidence yet. Routine.
